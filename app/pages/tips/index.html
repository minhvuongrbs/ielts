📋 11 IELTS Reading Question Types
🔤 1. Multiple Choice Practiced ▼
Choose the correct answer from 4 options (A, B, C, or D). Tests your ability to recognise specific points in a passage or to understand the main idea.
🇻🇳 Chọn đáp án đúng từ 4 lựa chọn (A, B, C hoặc D). Kiểm tra khả năng nhận ra các điểm cụ thể hoặc ý chính của đoạn văn.
  • Read the question stem carefully — identify key words
  • Find the matching paragraph first, then compare ALL options
  • Watch for qualifying words like 'always', 'never', 'not sure'
  • Eliminate distractors — options that are true but don't answer the question
  • Questions usually follow the order of the text

🔗 2. Matching Information Practiced ▼
You are given a number of statements and must find which paragraph (A, B, C, etc.) contains that information. Tests your ability to locate specific details across a long passage.
🇻🇳 Bạn được cho các phát biểu và phải tìm đoạn văn nào chứa thông tin đó. Kiểm tra khả năng xác định vị trí thông tin cụ thể trong bài đọc dài.
  • Read all the statements first before looking at the passage
  • Underline key words in each statement
  • Skim each paragraph looking for paraphrased matches — the text won't use the exact same words
  • A paragraph may match more than one statement, or may not be used at all
  • Don't be tricked by single word matches — check the full meaning
Practice: What is Happiness? · On the City Doorstep · European River Cruising
📑 3. Matching Headings Practiced ▼
Choose the best heading for each section of the passage from a list of headings. Tests your ability to identify the main idea of each section.
🇻🇳 Nối tiêu đề — Chọn tiêu đề phù hợp nhất cho mỗi đoạn văn từ danh sách. Kiểm tra khả năng xác định ý chính của mỗi đoạn.
  • Read the list of headings carefully first before reading the passage
  • Skim each section for its main idea — don't get distracted by details
  • The heading should summarise the WHOLE section, not just one sentence
  • Cross off headings as you use them — but remember some are distractors (not used)
  • If two headings seem to fit, choose the one that covers the broadest idea of the section

✏️ 6. Sentence Completion Practiced ▼
You are given incomplete sentences and must complete them using words taken directly from the reading passage. There is a word limit (e.g. no more than three words).
🇻🇳 Bạn được cho các câu chưa hoàn chỉnh và phải hoàn thành chúng bằng các từ lấy trực tiếp từ bài đọc. Có giới hạn số từ (ví dụ: không quá ba từ).
  • Read the instruction carefully — note the maximum number of words allowed
  • The words must come directly from the passage — don't change the form
  • Read the incomplete sentence first and predict what type of word is needed
  • Questions follow the order of information in the passage
  • Check that your completed sentence is grammatically correct
  • Count your words — exceeding the limit means zero marks for that question
Practice: The Complete Gardening Handbook
📝 7. Summary Completion Practiced ▼
Complete a summary of the passage by filling in blanks with words taken from the text (no more than 2–3 words). Tests your understanding of the main ideas.
🇻🇳 Hoàn thành bản tóm tắt bằng cách điền từ lấy từ bài đọc (không quá 2–3 từ). Kiểm tra khả năng hiểu ý chính.
  • Read the whole summary first to understand the overall meaning
  • Identify what type of word is needed (noun, verb, adjective)
  • Find the relevant section of the passage — summaries usually follow text order
  • Copy words exactly from the text — don't change the form
  • Check the word limit — 'no more than two or three words'

✅ 10. True / False / Not Given Practiced ▼
You are given statements and must decide if they agree with the information in the text (TRUE), contradict it (FALSE), or if there is no information about this (NOT GIVEN). Tests your ability to identify specific details and distinguish between what is stated, what is contradicted, and what is simply not mentioned.
🇻🇳 Bạn được cho các phát biểu và phải quyết định chúng đúng (TRUE), sai (FALSE), hay không có thông tin (NOT GIVEN) so với bài đọc. Kiểm tra khả năng phân biệt giữa thông tin được nêu, bị mâu thuẫn, và không được đề cập.
  • Read the statement carefully — look for absolute words like 'every', 'all', 'only', 'always'
  • TRUE means the text says exactly this (maybe in different words)
  • FALSE means the text says the opposite or something clearly different
  • NOT GIVEN means the text doesn't mention this at all — don't use your own knowledge
  • Be careful with partial matches — if only part of the statement is confirmed, it may be FALSE or NOT GIVEN
