🧬 The Happiness Equation (3 factors)
  • ~50% — Genetics — your "default" happiness level at birth
  • ~8–15% — Life circumstances (income, where you live)
  • ~35–42% — Thoughts & actions — the part YOU can control
🔑 Key ideas by paragraph
  • A — Happiness means different things to different people; common traits exist
  • B — Not money/looks/luck — deeper factors matter more
  • C — Money only matters when below the poverty line
  • D — Happy people think and act differently
  • E — Happiness improves both physical and mental health
  • F — Genetic "set-point" = ~50%; we return to baseline
  • G — Circumstances = only 8–15%
  • H — Thoughts/actions = up to 42%; you can control this
  • I — Happy people focus on good memories and practice forgiveness
  • J — Sadness is normal and even makes happiness brighter
  • K — Happiness can be trained — it's up to you
📌 IELTS Tip: Paragraph matching questions
  • Read the questions first, then scan for the matching paragraph
  • Look for paraphrasing — the test won't use the exact same words
  • Focus on the main idea of each paragraph, not just keywords
  • Paragraphs may be reused — don't cross them off
